Frequently asked questions

How is Lumen different from Amplitude or Mixpanel?

Lumen is built around an AI query layer and a unified data model. You get product analytics, experimentation, and warehouse sync in one tool — priced transparently, with no event caps.

Do you have a free tier?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for teams under 50k monthly tracked users. No credit card required to sign up.

Can I bring my own warehouse?

Absolutely. Lumen syncs with Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, and Databricks in both directions. Your data stays yours.

What about data privacy and compliance?

Lumen is SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR compliant. Enterprise customers can choose EU, US, or custom data residency.
